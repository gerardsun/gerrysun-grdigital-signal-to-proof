# Demo Data Use

All CSV files under `demo/data/` are synthetic and were constructed solely to demonstrate analytical behavior.

They do not represent a real company, customer, product, market, campaign, or transaction history.

The observational scenario intentionally contains self-selection and confounding so the engine has a reason to refuse causal language.

The randomized scenario intentionally contains pre/post treatment structure so the engine can demonstrate the additional evidence required for causal inference.
