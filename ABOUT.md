# About Gerry Sun / GRDigital

**Gerry H. Sun** is the founder of **GRDigital**, an end-to-end strategy, product, go-to-market, and AI/ML practice published at [geraldrobert.com](https://www.geraldrobert.com/).

My work sits between disciplines that are often separated inside organizations: business strategy, commercial economics, product and platform development, UX and service design, behavioral analytics, marketing, operations, decision rights, software, and market adoption.

That range is the point of Signal-to-Proof. Correlation and causal inference do not create business value in isolation. They become useful when the measurement architecture is connected to the decision being made, the economics of being wrong, the product behaviors that can actually be observed, the data needed to distinguish signal from confounding, and the operating action that follows.

Signal-to-Proof therefore begins with a decision and ends with a decision. Statistical modeling sits in the middle, where it belongs.

## Public practice context

GRDigital organizes its work across three integrated areas:

- **Dollar & Sense:** strategy, economics, operating model, value logic, and decision rights.
- **Product & GTM:** product ecosystems, experience, market strategy, commercialization, and adoption.
- **AI & ML Ecosystem:** software, knowledge and data systems, workflows, decision support, and intelligent products.

Signal-to-Proof is a technical demonstration of how those disciplines can converge around one measurement problem without collapsing correlation into causality.
